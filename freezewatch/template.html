Glossary
Freeze / blacklist
Tether (or Circle) blocks an address: it can no longer send the token. Funds stay on the address.
Unfreeze
The block is lifted.
Destroy (confiscation)
The whole balance of an already-frozen address is burned. The funds are gone, which is why it is shown as "burned".
Issue / mint
New tokens are created to Tether's treasury (supply goes up). Not related to freezes.
Redeem / burn
Tokens are removed from Tether's treasury (supply goes down). Not related to freezes.
Proposal, multisig, signatures x/y
Tether's token owner is a multisig wallet: an action is first proposed by one signer, then needs x of y signatures before it executes. "1/2 signed" means one more signature is needed.
Safe API vs on-chain queue
Most chains show a pending proposal on-chain. USDT0 chains use a Safe wallet whose queue lives off-chain in the Safe service, and signers may skip it, so some freezes are never announced.
No public proposal
The freeze was found on-chain but no matching published Safe proposal exists, so its delay is unknown.
No advance notice
The action executed in the same moment it was proposed (delay 0), so nobody could see it coming.
Delay
Time from the proposal being submitted to the action being executed. On TRON the execution time is inferred from the confirmations.
Stale
A proposal older than 7 days that never executed. Funds may still be on the address.
Cross-chain signal
An address frozen (or proposed for freezing) on one chain that still holds at least $1,000 on another chain where it is not blocked. The lag is the time until the second chain blocks it; "moved before" means its balance there dropped by more than 10% first.
Moved out before the freeze
Token transfers from the address in the 72 hours before the freeze executed. "In-window" = between the proposal being submitted and executed. Recipients are labelled from wallet-attribution where known.
Cluster
Batch: freezes on one chain within 10 minutes of each other. Cross-chain: one address frozen on several chains. Counterparty: frozen addresses sharing senders or recipients (labelled exchanges, bridges, protocols and busy hubs are ignored as connectors). A cluster is a lead, not proof of a common owner.
Reason
Why an address was frozen is rarely public. A reason is shown only from a curated entry with a public source (config/reasons.yaml) or as a hint from a wallet-attribution label (hack, scam, ransomware, sanctions ...), always with its source link. "Unknown" means no public reason is known to this tool.
OFAC SDN
The address is on the current OFAC SDN list of digital-currency addresses, according to a wallet-attribution label whose sources include ofac-sdn. Other "sanctioned" labels without that source are shown as hints only.
Pulse
Executions by hour and weekday (UTC), how long each signer takes to confirm, how often a signer gives the last signature, the monthly median delay per chain, and the number of open proposals over time.
Address page
#addr=<address> opens a shareable full page for one address with a copyable Markdown report (.md / .json download, or Print to PDF).
Third-party pull / sweeper candidate
The transaction that moved the money was sent by someone other than the frozen address (a bot or a contract pulled it). A contract that did this to two or more later-frozen addresses is listed as a sweeper candidate: a lead, not proof.
Allowance / EIP-7702
At proposal time, a spender with an ERC-20 / TRC-20 allowance (Permit2 included), or an EIP-7702 delegation of the account, can still move the funds even though the proposal is public. Shown on the address page and in the FREEZE INCOMING alert.
Pre-proposal flight
An address that received funds from a frozen address sends money on before any freeze proposal exists on it. Recipients (hop 1) and their recipients (hop 2) are watched.
Outcome classes
clean / racing / partial / weak are this tool's own heuristic over the 72 h before a freeze (see Analytics), not a finding about anyone's intent.
Labels
Entity labels come from the public wallet-attribution dataset (MIT) and its upstream sources (DefiLlama CEX lists, GraphSense, OFAC SDN and others). They are shown as "label from wallet-attribution", never as our own claim.
Sinks (excluded)
Zero address and token-contract addresses are blacklisted to burn mistaken sends; they are excluded from totals and lists.
USDT, USDT0, pre-USDT0 Arbitrum
USDT is Tether's own token; USDT0 is its omnichain version controlled by a different Safe wallet. On Arbitrum the token changed hands on 2025-01-28: earlier actions were done by Tether's multisig (shown as "Arbitrum USDT"), later ones by the USDT0 Safe.

Sent after freeze = token transfers whose recipient was blacklisted at the time; those funds are locked at once and are typically burned at the next destroy. Burned = amount in the DestroyedBlackFunds event. Still frozen = current balance of blacklisted addresses (frozen funds cannot move), refreshed incrementally. Frozen $ = burned amount if later destroyed, else current balance, else last balance seen while frozen. Raw aggregates: analytics.json.
